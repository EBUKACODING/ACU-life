# Campus Mapping and Reference Guide

The aim is to make a compact game map that feels like ACU without trying to reproduce every building immediately.

## 1. Start with a top-down map
Use the satellite screenshot as a broad reference. Mark:
- Main entrance and other permitted access points.
- Major roads and footpaths.
- Important buildings and open spaces.
- Landmarks that help players recognize where they are.
- The boundary between the campus and nearby surroundings.

Treat satellite measurements as approximate until checked against real-world references.

## 2. Collect ground-level references responsibly
When walking around areas where photography is allowed:
- Take wide shots of buildings, paths, intersections, and open spaces.
- Capture several angles of a building so its shape is understandable.
- Note the name or a temporary label for each location.
- Record approximate walking time between landmarks.
- Avoid including identifiable people in photos.
- Do not photograph private rooms, security arrangements, access controls, or restricted locations.
- Follow university rules and ask permission where required.

## 3. Keep a simple landmark record
For each location, record:
- Landmark ID (for example, BLD-01).
- Common name.
- Type (classroom, hostel, hall, road, field, etc.).
- Nearby landmarks.
- Approximate position on your sketch map.
- Reference photo filenames, if permitted.
- Notes about shape, colour, entrances, and surrounding paths.

## 4. Build a greybox before detailed models
Represent buildings with simple boxes and paths with flat surfaces. Check:
- Are the distances believable?
- Can the player navigate between landmarks?
- Are roads and paths connected?
- Do buildings have the correct general orientation?

Only add detailed models after the layout feels right.

## 5. Suggested folder structure for reference material
Keep local reference material organized, and only commit files you have permission to share:

```
Reference/
  Maps/
  Photos/
  Landmark-Notes/
  Sketches/
```

Do not upload sensitive campus information or photos of people without appropriate permission.
