# ACU Life

An open-world campus-life simulation inspired by Ajayi Crowther University (ACU), focused on a compact, recognizable version of the campus and its nearby surroundings.

## Project vision

Build a small, explorable campus where players can walk around, discover landmarks, meet fictional student characters, attend activities, and take on simple missions.

The first goal is a **playable vertical slice**, not a huge open world.

## Proposed technology

- **Engine:** Unity
- **Language:** C#
- **Target prototype:** Windows PC first
- **Art direction:** Stylized 3D, with recognizable campus landmarks and lightweight assets

These are starting recommendations and can be changed before implementation.

## First playable milestone

1. A third-person player controller.
2. A small test area with paths and a few placeholder buildings.
3. Basic camera and collision.
4. A simple interaction prompt.
5. One fictional NPC and one short objective.

## Repository guide

- `docs/roadmap.md` — development milestones and feature priorities.
- `docs/campus-mapping.md` — how to collect reference material and map the campus responsibly.

## Mapping principle

Use satellite imagery and your own permitted reference photos to understand the layout. Record approximate positions, paths, entrances, and building shapes. Avoid photographing people, private spaces, or restricted areas without permission.

## Development approach

Prototype with simple shapes first. Replace placeholders with detailed models only after movement, scale, and navigation feel right.
